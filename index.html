❗️ The basics
  • When you leave the house, don't leave any food or valuable items within reach (dining, work, coffee tables).
  • When you leave the house, close all room doors.
  • After a walk, take off her harness.
  • Joy is allowed everywhere except the bed (though she may still climb on it, mostly in winter 😅).
💛 Personality
  • She sleeps quite a lot during the day — around 12–14 hours.
  • It's better not to disturb her when she is resting or sleeping.
  • Her activity level increases after walks; she'll bring you her toys.
  • Talk to her — she understands a lot. We mix Ukrainian and English; she pays attention to intonation.
  • She likes laying on the balcony for short sessions (it gets too hot).
  • She can be protective of her food and space — sometimes growls or barks. Don't take it personally; she won't bite. She was the smallest of her litter and had to "conquer" food from her siblings.
🍽 Feeding
  • 💧 Always keep water in her bowl. Rinse and refill 1–2 times a day.
  • 🍖 All dog food is stored in the cupboard under the sink (a little to the right). Salmon oil and opened wet food go in the fridge.
  • Daily totals: 90 g dry food + 50–60 g wet food.
  • Use her special spoon (rubber-wrapped, kept near the sink) to serve wet food.
  • Wash her bowl after every meal with the dedicated brush. Once a day after dinner, pour boiling water over it (no detergent, just water + brush).
  • Chewing sticks: once every 1–2 days (same cupboard).
  • Walking treats are kept near the entrance, top shelf.
📍 Walks
  • ❗️ Don't let her pick anything off the ground — she may try to grab food or trash. Be especially attentive near benches and picnic areas.
  • Out our building, go right toward Turia park (we try to avoid too many steps).
  • For walks longer than 30 min, bring water — there are fountains in the park (silicone bowl is near the entrance).
  • Up to 10 treats per walk from the pouch.
  • Give her time to sniff — bushes, trees, etc.
  • She walks well on leash and enjoys off-leash time in safe spots (only if you're confident).
  • She loves sticks — she'll bring a good one back to you many times.
  • She can be triggered by sudden loud sounds and movements (running children, motorcycles). Cross the street and redirect her with voice + treats.
  • Sometimes she stops and refuses to walk — gently pull the leash in the right direction.
  • After every walk, wipe her paws (wipes are on the shoe shelf), even short ones.
🏠 When alone at home
  • She may "misbehave" a little — lately she sometimes gets into the dry trash bin.
  • Trash with food leftovers should always go in the mixed-waste bin (the big one).
  • When you leave, you can put the smaller trash bin on the balcony.
